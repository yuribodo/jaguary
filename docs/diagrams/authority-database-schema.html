PHYSICAL SCHEMA · ECONOMIC CORE

The transaction spine, column by column

Five tables enforce the binding from human mandate and merchant checkout through replay protection, reserved authorization, and the single payment attempt.

Why these five

They are the atomic path from immutable authority and merchant-authored economics to replay prevention, reservation, and payment. Other domains feed this spine but do not replace its checks.

Reading the omissions

Each final row reports hidden columns. Identity, credential ownership, signatures, idempotency, evidence hashes, timestamps, and indexes remain in the Drizzle schema even when omitted here.
