## Executive Summary

Yuno has raised **$80M total** across three funding rounds (Seed: $10M in 2022, Series A: $25M in March 2024 at $150M valuation, Series B: $45M in August 2026 with undisclosed valuation) from tier-1 global investors (Andreessen Horowitz, Tiger Global, DST Global, Kaszek, Monashees) and strategic regional investors (Global PayTech Ventures, QuantumLight Capital, Rasmal Ventures, Further Ventures, GrowthX Capital). Investor composition reveals **four strategic priorities**: (1) **AI-native payments infrastructure** (QuantumLight Capital — Nik Storonsky/Revolut, AI-focused VC; Series B proceeds allocated to R&D, next-gen payment technologies, AI agents), (2) **GCC/Middle East expansion** (Rasmal Ventures — Qatar Investment Authority-backed, Further Ventures — Abu Dhabi sovereign-backed, GrowthX Capital — Hamad Al-Hajri-backed; Yuno establishing Qatar as GCC regional hub), (3) **Enterprise payments infrastructure** (DST Global, Tiger Global, a16z — all enterprise-focused; Yuno targeting $100B annual transaction volume within 12 months of Series B), (4) **LatAm roots with global ambition** (Kaszek, Monashees — LatAm-focused; Yuno founded in Bogotá, expanding to 190+ countries). Yuno has **no acquisitions, no investments in startups, no incubated products** — corporate strategy is **partnership-led** (dLocal, Prosa, Tap Payments, Onafriq, Flutterwave, Triple-A, Conduit, Crypto.com, BVNK, PRECISION) and **hackathon sponsorship** (NextWave Hackathon 2026 with Nauta, OpenAI support, $25K prize pool, 4 cities: São Paulo, Bogotá, Buenos Aires, Mexico City). Investor statements emphasize **AI-native architecture as moat** ("Yuno differentiates through an AI-native architecture that deploys autonomous agents rather than bolting AI onto existing stacks" — Javier Perez, Global PayTech Ventures), **GCC market opportunity** ("The Gulf is now one of the fastest-growing payments markets in the world" — Rasmal Ventures), **financial discipline** ("pair that global ambition with real financial discipline" — Rasmal Ventures), **enterprise infrastructure** ("foundational payment infrastructure provider for large global enterprises" — Ebrun). For hackathon, top 5 opportunities aligned with Yuno's strategic direction: (1) **AI agents for payment operations** (Payments Concierge extension, aligns with QuantumLight/AI focus), (2) **GCC payment corridor optimizer** (Mada, KNET, NAPS optimization, aligns with Rasmal/Further/GrowthX GCC focus), (3) **Stablecoin cross-border settlement** (Conduit partnership extension, aligns with next-gen payment technologies), (4) **Enterprise subscription billing optimizer** (network tokenization, multi-acquirer portability, aligns with DST/Tiger/a16z enterprise focus), (5) **LatAm payment method optimizer** (Pix, Boleto, SPEI, OXXO optimization, aligns with Kaszek/Monashees LatAm focus). Yuno's capital history tells us company is heading toward **AI-native global payments infrastructure** — not just payment orchestration, but autonomous payment operations, agentic commerce, stablecoin rails, enterprise financial infrastructure.
