## Executive Summary

Yuno's AI strategy has evolved from ML-powered optimization (2024) to AI-native autonomous operations (2026). The company has deployed two productized AI agents (NOVA for payment recovery, Payments Concierge for operations monitoring) and is actively hiring for AI/LLM engineering roles. Executive statements, job postings, and product launches indicate Yuno is pursuing **autonomous payment operations** as the end state, with AI agents as the primary architectural pattern. The company differentiates through "AI-native architecture" (AI embedded in core workflows from inception) rather than "AI-powered" features bolted onto legacy infrastructure. Evidence suggests Yuno views AI as fundamental to unit economics ("AI has changed the economics of building this company") and is preparing infrastructure for "agentic commerce" future where AI agents initiate transactions, not just humans. #### A. AI-Assisted Payments
**DEFINITION:** AI helps humans make better payment decisions (e.g., dashboards with ML insights, alerts with anomaly detection).

**EVIDENCE FOR:**
- AI payment analytics provides insights faster than human analysts [157]
- Natural language querying via Payment Concierge [157][159]
- Intelligent routing recommendations [148][150][151][157][158]

**VERDICT:** AI-assisted payments is baseline — Yuno has moved beyond this to autonomous operations.

#### B. AI-Optimized Payments
**DEFINITION:** AI optimizes payment routing, fraud detection, approval rates (ML models improve outcomes, but humans still manage operations).

**EVIDENCE FOR:**

- NOVA autonomously contacts customers and recovers payments — human doesn't manage recovery process [136]
- Ortega: "This is what agentic infrastructure looks like for payments" — frames as infrastructure, not optimization tool [159]

**VERDICT:** AI-optimized payments is part of strategy, but Yuno has moved beyond optimization to autonomous agents.

---

#### C. AI Agents
**DEFINITION:** AI agents are primary architectural pattern — autonomous systems that perceive, decide, and act within defined boundaries.

**EVIDENCE FOR:**
- Two productized AI agents deployed (NOVA, Payments Concierge) [136][137][159][170][171][172]

- "NOVA is just the beginning" — signals more AI agents coming [136]

**EVIDENCE AGAINST:**
- AI agents are means to end, not end state — end state is autonomous payment operations
- AI agents serve specific functions (recovery, monitoring) — not general-purpose agents

**VERDICT:** AI agents are primary architectural pattern — but they serve larger goal of autonomous payment operations.

---

#### D. Autonomous Payment Operations
**DEFINITION:** Payment operations run autonomously with minimal human intervention — AI agents monitor, diagnose, and fix issues within configured permissions; humans focus on strategy, not execution.

**EVIDENCE FOR:**
- Payments Concierge "monitors, analyzes, and acts across a merchant's full payment operations" — autonomous optimization within configured permissions [137][159][167][170][171][172]

**EVIDENCE AGAINST:**
- Some actions still require human approval (Payments Concierge "implements the fix once you approve it") [170]
- AI governance framework includes "escalate-to-human" tier — not fully autonomous

**VERDICT:** **Autonomous payment operations is the end state** — AI agents are the means to achieve it.

**DEFINITION:** Yuno's AI strategy is something other than A-D (e.g., AI-native OS for payments, agentic commerce infrastructure, AI-powered financial infrastructure).

**EVIDENCE FOR:**

**EVIDENCE AGAINST:**
- "AI-native OS" and "agentic commerce infrastructure" are broader visions that encompass autonomous payment operations
- Autonomous payment operations is concrete near-term goal; "AI-native OS" is aspirational long-term vision

**VERDICT:** **"AI-native operating system for payments" is the vision; autonomous payment operations is the near-term execution strategy.**

**PRIMARY INTEREST:** **D. Autonomous Payment Operations** (with C. AI Agents as architectural pattern)

**EVIDENCE SUMMARY:**
