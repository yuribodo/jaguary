## Executive Summary

Yuno's customers are primarily **enterprise merchants with complex cross-border payment operations** (McDonald's, Rappi, inDrive, NetEase Games, GoFundMe, Whop, Crypto.com, Viva Aerobus, Livelo, Vibra/Premmia, Reserva, Petrobras, Avianca, Sympla) operating across fragmented payment landscapes (Latin America, APAC, EMEA, GCC, Africa). The core problem Yuno solves is **payment infrastructure complexity** — merchants using 20+ PSPs across 50+ countries can't manually optimize routing, monitor provider performance, or recover failed payments at scale. Yuno's value proposition is **multi-PSP orchestration** (1,000+ payment methods, 460+ integrations, 190+ countries) with **AI-driven optimization** (8 percentage point authorization rate uplift, 75% payment recovery rate via NOVA, 80% analyst time reduction via Payments Concierge). Quantifiable customer outcomes include: inDrive (90% approval rate, 4.5% recovery rate uplift), Rappi (80% analyst time reduction, 8% recovery rate lift), Viva Aerobus (75% failed payment recovery, $300+ per transaction recovered), Livelo (50% failed transaction recovery, 5% approval rate increase), Vibra/Premmia (30+ percentage point new-user approval rate increase to 80%), Reserva (4 percentage point approval rate increase within 3 months). Recurring pain patterns across customers: issuer-acquirer pairing mismatches, single-PSP dependency, payment method fragmentation, failed payment recovery, cross-border complexity, subscription churn from credential decay. Yuno's strongest customer segments are **mobility/ride-hailing** (inDrive, Rappi, Uber), **travel** (Viva Aerobus, Avianca), **gaming** (NetEase Games), **marketplaces** (GoFundMe, Whop), **retail/e-commerce** (McDonald's, Reserva, Livelo, Vibra/Premmia), **crypto** (Crypto.com). For hackathon, top 10 customer problems: (1) issuer-acquirer pairing mismatches, (2) failed payment recovery, (3) subscription churn from credential decay, (4) cross-border payment complexity, (5) payment method fragmentation in emerging markets, (6) real-time provider monitoring, (7) cost-aware routing optimization, (8) local payment method enablement, (9) fraud vs. false decline balancing, (10) multi-PSP reconciliation. If Yuno could solve only three customer problems over next two years: (1) **issuer-acquirer pairing mismatches** (root cause of persistent authorization failures, requires multi-PSP data), (2) **failed payment recovery at scale** (9-20% revenue lost to payment failures, AI agents can recover 75%), (3) **subscription churn from credential decay** (50% of subscription churn tied to failed payments, network tokenization with multi-acquirer portability is structural solution). ## 2. Customer Use Cases

### Detailed Use Cases by Customer

#### 1. Rappi — Payment Method Fragmentation & Provider Monitoring

**BEFORE YUNO:**
- **PROBLEM:** Rappi needed to integrate alternative payment methods (APMs) across 9 Latin American countries without lengthy development processes. [227]
- **PROBLEM:** Consumer preferences for APMs vary by region (Pix in Brazil, SPEI in Mexico, OXXO cash payments, local cards, wallets) — difficult to keep up with regional preferences. [227]
- **PROBLEM:** Rappi had 20+ payment providers — manually monitoring each provider for outages, approval rate drops, routing issues took 5-10 minutes per incident, resulting in lost transactions. [227]
- **PROBLEM:** What would have taken Rappi 12-18 months to develop internally (multi-PSP integration, smart routing, monitoring) was blocking growth. [227]

**YUNO:**
- **SOLUTION:** Yuno's payment orchestration platform provided instant access to 300+ payment methods (now 1,000+) through single no-code API integration. [227]
- **SOLUTION:** Yuno's Monitors feature alerts Rappi when one of its 20 payment providers experiences issues — real-time monitoring. [227]
- **SOLUTION:** Yuno's Smart Routing technology redirects transactions to most efficient alternative provider automatically — millisecond response time vs. 5-10 minutes manual. [227]
- **CAPABILITIES USED:** Payment orchestration, Smart Routing, Monitors, no-code API integration, 300+ payment methods. [227]
- **PAYMENT METHODS:** Pix (Brazil), Boleto (Brazil), SPEI (Mexico), OXXO (Mexico), local cards, wallets, cash payments. [227]
- **COUNTRIES:** 9 Latin American countries (Colombia, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Ecuador, Costa Rica, Panama). [227]

**OUTCOME:**
- **RESULT:** Integration time reduced from months to minutes — what would have taken 12-18 months internally achieved in few months with Yuno. [227]

- **Business impact:** 9-20% revenue lost to payment failures industry-wide — NOVA recovers up to 75% of failed payments on contacted customers, on $500M volume with 10% failure rate = $37.5M recovered revenue at 75% recovery. [224][234][240][241][243][246]
- **Customer evidence:** Viva Aerobus (75% recovery, $300+ per transaction), Livelo (50% recovery), Rappi (8% lift), inDrive (4.5% uplift), Reserva, Petrobras, all customers affected by failed payments. [224][234][241][246]
- **Difficulty for customers to solve themselves:** Requires AI agents (NOVA) to contact customers within minutes of failure, guide them to complete transaction — manual recovery is slow, expensive, inconsistent. [224][234][241][246]
- **Infrastructure required:** AI agents (NOVA), multi-channel outreach (WhatsApp, voice, email, SMS, push notifications), root cause identification, personalization — Yuno already has NOVA (up to 75% recovery), needs to expand to more channels, more languages, more personalization. [224][234][241][246]
- **AI opportunity:** AI could expand NOVA to more channels (email, SMS, push notifications), more languages (70+ currently), more personalization (customer-specific scripts, optimal outreach timing, preferred channel per customer). [224][234][241][246]

**EVIDENCE:** Viva Aerobus recovered 75% of failed payments, $300+ per transaction; Livelo recovered 50% of failed transactions; Yuno recovered $5B+ platform-wide over past 12 months. [226][229][230][234][246]

#### 3. Subscription Churn from Credential Decay

**WHY THIS PROBLEM:**
- **Business impact:** 50% of subscription churn tied to failed payments — most from credential decay (cards get reissued, banks update account details, static vaults don't keep up), on $100M subscription revenue with 10% churn = $1M lost revenue, 50% from credential decay = $500K recoverable revenue. [245]
- **Customer evidence:** Whop, NetEase Games, GoFundMe, SaaS customers, subscription businesses all affected by subscription churn from credential decay. [245]
- **Difficulty for customers to solve themselves:** Requires network tokenization with multi-acquirer portability (tokens survive PSP changes and card updates), Card Account Updater (automatic credential refresh) — static vaults don't keep up, cards get reissued, banks update account details. [188][190][191][192][245]
- **Infrastructure required:** Network tokenization, multi-acquirer portability, Card Account Updater, AI-powered churn predictor — Yuno already has network tokenization with multi-acquirer portability, needs to enhance AI-powered churn prediction. [188][190][191][192][245]
- **AI opportunity:** AI could predict which subscriptions are at risk of churn from credential decay (card expiration, reissuance), proactively prompt customers to update credentials (email, SMS, push notifications, WhatsApp), optimize retry timing (when to retry failed subscription payments for highest success rate). [245]

**EVIDENCE:** 50% of subscription churn tied to credential decay, network tokenization with multi-acquirer portability is structural solution. [188][190][191][192][245]
