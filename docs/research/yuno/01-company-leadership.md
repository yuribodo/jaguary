## Founding & Original Problem

### Founding Story

**FACT:** Yuno was founded in January 2022 in Bogotá, Colombia by Juan Pablo Ortega and Juli án Núñ¿¿¿, both former Rappi executives. [20][21][22][25][26][27][42][45][47]

**FACT:** The company emerged from firsthand experience with payment infrastructure challenges while scaling Rappi across Latin America. [43][47][48][51][52]

**Original Problem:**

**FACT:** While at Rappi, Ortega and Núñ¿¿¿ encountered severe payment fragmentation across Latin American markets. Each country required different payment processors, with 30-40% transaction decline rates consistently across Colombia, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, and Chile. [48][52]

**FACT:** Rappi's solution was to connect to multiple processors in each country, build retry logic, and hire hundreds of people to manage the complexity — the payments team became one of the largest functions in the company. [52]

**INFERENCE:** The founders recognized that if Rappi — a well-funded unicorn — struggled with this complexity, smaller companies expanding globally faced insurmountable barriers. [48][51][52]

**FACT:** According to Juan Pablo Ortega: "Integrating payment providers in each new market is very expensive, complex and time-consuming for internal technical teams." [51]

**FACT:** According to Juli án Núñ¿¿¿: "When Rappi started in Mexico, we realized the processor we used in Colombia didn't even exist there. We had to connect to another company. And they also declined 30-40% of payments. We went to Brazil—identical story. Argentina, Chile, everywhere." [52]

**INFERENCE:** The founding insight was that payment infrastructure fragmentation was a universal problem for any company operating across multiple markets, not just a Latin American issue. [43][48][51][52]
